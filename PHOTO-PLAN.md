# Photo placement guide

These nine labeled slots are temporary. Their illustrations are layout placeholders, not photographs. Replace each image with your chosen photo and update its caption and alternative text.

| Page | Suggested subject | Placeholder file |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `index.qmd` | The team together outdoors, at NAU, or during a lab gathering. | `lab-group.svg` |
| `people.qmd` | A relaxed group photo with current lab members, ideally in your shared workspace. | `team-in-context.svg` |
| `research.qmd` | Lab members working through forecasts at a whiteboard or sharing a screen. | `research-in-action.svg` |
| `research.qmd` | A workshop, partner meeting, or field visit with permission from everyone pictured. | `public-health-partners.svg` |
| `culture.qmd` | A candid lab meeting, mentoring conversation, or whiteboard discussion. | `lab-conversation.svg` |
| `culture.qmd` | A lab outing, shared meal, conference break, or hike around Flagstaff. | `lab-life.svg` |
| `opportunities.qmd` | Students collaborating in an NAU workspace or a welcoming campus scene. | `joining-the-lab.svg` |
| `publications.qmd` | A lab member presenting a poster or giving a conference talk. | `sharing-research.svg` |
| `contact.qmd` | Your building, a recognizable NAU campus landmark, or the view near the lab. | `flagstaff-campus.svg` |

Placeholder assets are in `images/web/placeholders/`. Wide slots use a 2:1 crop; compact slots use a 3:1 crop on desktop and 2:1 on phones. Keep important faces near the center, or adjust `object-position` for the final image.

When replacing a slot, keep the `.photo-slot` wrapper for its spacing, replace the image path and `fig-alt`, and remove the “Suggested photo” text or replace it with a real caption. Slots can also be removed entirely if a page feels better without them.
